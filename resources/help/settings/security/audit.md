The security audit log is a record of important actions in each workspace: sign-ins, membership changes (who belongs to the workspace), configuration changes and data changes. Old entries are deleted once a day.

- **Keep the security audit log** — When on, these actions are recorded, and each workspace sees its own log. When off, nothing new is recorded. Entries already saved stay until they are older than the days to keep them.
- **Days to keep the security log** — How long entries are kept. This is the normal time for every workspace. A workspace may choose fewer days, but not more.
- **Fewest days a workspace may choose** — The shortest time a workspace may set for its own log. It cannot be more than the days to keep the log. Some rules or laws ask you to keep security records for a minimum time.

**Tip:** Keep it on. It helps you find out what happened after a problem.
