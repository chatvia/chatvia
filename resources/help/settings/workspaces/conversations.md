Controls how long conversations are kept before they are deleted.

- **Days to keep conversations** — A conversation is deleted, with its files, after this many days without new activity. A conversation that people still use is kept. A check runs once a day. A lower number also applies to conversations that already exist.
- **Fewest days a workspace may choose** — Each workspace can choose to keep its conversations for fewer days, in its own security settings. This is the lowest number it may choose. It cannot be more than **Days to keep conversations**.

**Tip:** Leave a field empty to use the default shown in it.
