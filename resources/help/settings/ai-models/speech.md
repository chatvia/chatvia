Speech to text turns voice input, and audio and video sources, into text.

The list shows the service in use, and whether each provider is connected. A provider that is not connected is greyed out: add its key in **AI providers** first. When you pick a new service and save, the app calls the provider once to check its key. If the call fails, nothing is saved and the error says why.

- **Speech to text** — **Scaleway AI Whisper** or **OpenAI Whisper**; both use a Whisper model. **Off** turns these off and hides audio and video files from the sources.
- **Scaleway AI model** and **OpenAI model** (under **Advanced**) — The Whisper model each one uses. Leave them blank for the defaults.

**Tip:** Not sure? Pick the provider you already use for chat.
