Chooses the service that sends this app's emails, such as sign-up confirmations and invitations, and the sender that people see.

- **Send email with** — The email service to use. **Log only** sends nothing: emails are only written to a log file, so people cannot confirm a sign-up or accept an invitation. **Sendmail** uses this server's own mail program and needs no other fields.
- The fields under it (for example **Host** and **Password** for an SMTP server, or **API key** for Resend) — They change with your choice. Copy the values from your account at that service. **Get a key** opens the page where you create a key. Keys, tokens and passwords are stored encrypted and never shown again. Leave such a field blank to keep it.
- **Sender address** and **Sender name** — The "From" address and name on every email. Use an address that your email service allows you to send from.

**Tip:** After you save, click **Send a test email** at the top of the page. It sends an email to your own address.
