# Pinewood Outdoor: guided trips and courses 2026

Every year our team leads small groups on trips and teaches short courses. The trips are made for people who want to learn, not for experts. Our guides are certified mountain leaders or members of our test team, and every group has a first aider.

You book trips and courses online in **Trips & courses**, or in any shop. Trail Club members can book two weeks before everyone else.

## How the trips work

### Group size and guides

A trip runs with at least 4 and at most 10 people. There is one guide for every five people, so a full group has two guides. If fewer than 4 people have booked 21 days before the start, we cancel the trip and refund you in full, or you move to another date.

### What is included

The trip price includes the guides, all camp site and hut fees, the group gear (tarps, stoves, gas, pots, first aid, maps), and dinner and breakfast on every night out. It does not include travel to the meeting point, lunch, drinks, or your personal gear.

### Personal gear

Each trip below lists the personal gear you need. Trip members get 15% off that gear in our shops in the four weeks before the trip; show your booking at the till. You can also rent it, and on some trips we lend gear for free, as noted.

### Fitness and age

Every trip has a level from 1 to 4.

| Level | What it means |
|---|---|
| 1 | Up to 4 hours of walking a day, on paths, with little climbing. |
| 2 | Up to 6 hours a day, some steep paths, about 600 m of climbing a day. |
| 3 | Up to 7 hours a day, rough ground, up to 1,000 m of climbing a day, carrying all your gear. |
| 4 | Winter conditions, snow, cold nights. You must have done a level 3 trip or similar before. |

The minimum age is 16. Young people of 16 or 17 need a signed form from a parent, and on level 3 and 4 trips they must come with an adult.

### Cancellation

If you cancel more than 30 days before the start, you get a full refund minus 25 for the booking. Between 30 and 14 days, you get 50% back. Less than 14 days before the start, we cannot refund you, but you can give your place to someone else for free. If we cancel a trip because of dangerous weather, you get a full refund or a new date.

We advise travel insurance that covers walking and, for level 4, winter activities. Our trips do not include insurance.

## Trips

### First night outdoors

Level 1 · 2 days, 1 night · 169 per person

This trip is for people who have never slept in a tent. We walk about three hours along the lake to a small camp site, set up camp together, cook, and walk back the next morning. Lena from our test team leads most dates; she shows how to pitch a tent, how to stay warm at night and how to pack a backpack.

- **Meeting point:** the Lakeside shop, at 9:00 on Saturday.
- **Dates:** 9 May, 6 June, 4 July, 5 September.
- **Gear we lend for free:** a Trail 2 tent (shared by two people), a sleeping mat and a backpack.
- **Gear you bring:** a sleeping bag with a comfort temperature of 5 °C or lower, a waterproof jacket, walking shoes, a headlamp and a warm layer.

### Lakes and ridges

Level 2 · 3 days, 2 nights · 349 per person

Three days on the ridge path above the three lakes, with nights at camp sites with water and toilets. Each day we walk five to six hours, and we carry our own gear but no food; the food goes ahead with a car.

- **Meeting point:** the city centre shop, at 8:00 on Friday.
- **Dates:** 22 May, 19 June, 21 August, 18 September.
- **Gear you bring:** a tent for two (or share one with another member), a sleeping bag with a comfort temperature of 0 °C or lower, a sleeping mat with an R-value of at least 3, a backpack of 38 to 48 litres, rain gear, and walking boots.

### High route

Level 3 · 5 days, 4 nights · 690 per person

Our hardest summer trip: five days across the high plateau, wild camping on two nights and sleeping in mountain huts on the other two. We carry everything, including food for two days. The route goes over two passes of more than 2,000 m.

- **Meeting point:** the train station at Fennhausen, at 10:00 on Sunday. The train from the city takes about two hours.
- **Dates:** 12 July, 9 August.
- **Gear you bring:** a light tent (the Trail 1 or the Trail 2 is ideal), a down sleeping bag with a comfort temperature of -4 °C or lower, a sleeping mat with an R-value of at least 3, a backpack of 48 to 60 litres, a three-layer waterproof jacket, walking boots with a stiff sole, and two headlamp batteries or a power bank.

### Winter plateau

Level 4 · 3 days, 2 nights · 595 per person

Two nights in tents on snow, at about 1,800 m. We walk on snowshoes, build a camp with snow walls, and learn how to stay warm and dry when it is well below freezing. Nights can reach -15 °C.

- **Meeting point:** the Airport shop, at 7:00 on Thursday. A minibus takes the group to the start; the trip price includes it.
- **Dates:** 15 January, 12 February, 5 March.
- **Gear we lend for free:** a Summit 2 tent (shared by two people), snowshoes and an avalanche transceiver.
- **Gear you bring:** a sleeping bag with a comfort temperature of -11 °C or lower, two sleeping mats with a total R-value of at least 5, a backpack of 60 litres, a down jacket, insulated gloves and a spare pair, and winter boots.

The winter plateau trip is not run when the avalanche warning level is 3 or higher. The guide decides on the morning of the start, and you get a full refund or a new date.

## Courses

Courses take place in our shops or in the workshop, and on some courses in the park next to the Lakeside shop.

| Course | Length | Where | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pitching and packing | 2 hours | Park next to the Lakeside shop | Free |
| Map and compass | 1 day | City centre shop, then outdoors | 35 |
| Gear repair at home | 3 hours | The workshop | 25 |
| Cooking outdoors | 3 hours | Park next to the Lakeside shop | 30 |
| Winter camping | 2 days | Classroom, then a night on snow | 120 |

### Pitching and packing

On the first Saturday of every month from April to October, at 10:00. We practise with several of our tents, from the Trail 1 to the Basecamp 4, and show how to pack a backpack so the weight sits close to your back. No booking is needed; just come.

### Map and compass

You learn to read a topographic map, to take a bearing with a compass, and to find your place when the path is gone. In the afternoon we walk a short route in the hills with only map and compass. Bring a compass with a baseplate, or borrow one from us.

### Gear repair at home

Our workshop team shows you how to patch a hole in a tent or a jacket, replace a broken pole section, fix a zip slider, and clean and proof a waterproof jacket. You take home a small repair kit. The price is taken off your next repair in the workshop if you use it within three months.

### Cooking outdoors

We cook a three-course meal on small gas stoves and talk about planning food for a trip, keeping water clean, and leaving no trace. All food is included. Tell us about allergies when you book.

### Winter camping

The first day is in the classroom: layers, sleeping warm, keeping gas working in the cold, and the basics of avalanche safety. The second day we go up, build a camp and sleep one night on snow. The course is a good step before the Winter plateau trip, and it counts as the experience you need for level 4.

## Private groups

Schools, companies and clubs can book any trip or course as a private group, on a date that suits them. The price for a private group is the trip price for 8 people, also when fewer come, plus 10% for a group of 9 or 10. Ask our business team for an offer.
