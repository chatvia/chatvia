Options that help you find problems in this install. They are off by default.

- **Logs page** — Shows **Logs** in the sidebar. There, admins read the log files of this install (the messages the app writes when something goes wrong) and can clear them. While it is off, the page is hidden and its address does not open.

**Tip:** Turn it on while you look for a problem, then turn it off again. Log files can hold email addresses and other details of the people who use this install.
