# Pinewood Outdoor: help centre

Pinewood Outdoor sells tents, sleeping bags, backpacks and clothes for hiking and camping. We have three shops and an online store. This page answers the questions our customers ask most often. If you cannot find an answer here, our support team is happy to help.

## About us

Pinewood Outdoor started in 2009 as a small rental shop next to a mountain lake. Today we design our own products and test every one of them on real trips before we sell it. Our team has twenty-four people: designers, testers, shop staff and the support team.

We believe good gear should last for many years. That is why we repair what we sell, we sell spare parts, and we buy back used gear in good condition.

## Orders

### How to place an order

1. Add the products you want to your basket.
2. Open the basket and check the sizes and colours.
3. Click **Checkout** and sign in, or continue as a guest.
4. Enter your delivery address and choose a delivery option.
5. Pay with a card, PayPal or a gift card.

You get an email with your order number within a few minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder first, then contact us.

### Changing or cancelling an order

You can change or cancel an order for one hour after you place it. Open **My orders** in your account and click **Change** or **Cancel**. After one hour the order goes to our warehouse and we cannot change it any more. You can still return the items when they arrive.

### Paying

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Pinewood gift cards. We charge your card when the order leaves the warehouse, not when you place it. If an item is out of stock, we do not charge you for it.

Business customers can also pay by invoice. The invoice is due 30 days after delivery. Ask our business team to open an invoice account for you.

When your parcel leaves the warehouse, we send you an email with a tracking link. You can also find the link in **My orders**. The tracking information can take up to 12 hours to appear.

Business customers can follow their orders from their own systems. Ask the business team for an API key, then call our order service:

```bash
curl https://api.pinewood.example/v1/orders/PW-104233 \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY"
```

The answer tells you the status of the order, the parcels and their tracking numbers.

### Customs and taxes

Prices include VAT for every country in the European Union. For the United Kingdom, Norway and Switzerland, we pay the customs fees for you, so there is nothing more to pay when the parcel arrives.

## Returns and refunds

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused, clean, and in its original packaging with all labels. Gift cards, underwear, socks and personalised items cannot be returned.

### How to start a return

1. Sign in to your account and open **My orders**.
2. Choose the order and click **Return an item**.
3. Choose the items and tell us why you return them.
4. Print the return label and stick it on the parcel.
5. Drop the parcel at any post office or at one of our shops.

Returns are free within the European Union. From other countries, the return costs 7.95, which we take from your refund.

### Refunds

We check every return within three working days after it arrives. Refunds go back to the payment method you used. A card refund takes five to ten working days to show on your statement, depending on your bank. A PayPal refund usually arrives within one day.

We refund the delivery costs only when the item was faulty or we sent the wrong one.

### Exchanges

To change a size or a colour, return the item and place a new order. This is the fastest way to get the item you want, because we do not have to wait for the return first.

## Warranty and repairs

Every Pinewood product has a warranty of three years against faults in materials and making. Tents and backpacks have a warranty of five years. The warranty does not cover normal wear, damage from accidents, or damage from wrong use or care.

### What to do when something breaks

Send us a photo of the problem and your order number through the contact form. We usually answer within one working day. When the fault is covered, we repair the product for free, or we replace it if a repair is not possible.

### Repairs after the warranty

Our repair workshop also repairs products after the warranty ends, and products from other brands. We repair zips, seams, buckles, tent poles and holes in fabric. You get a price before we start, and most repairs take one to two weeks.

## Product care

Good care makes your gear last longer and keeps it working well.

### Tents

- Always dry your tent fully before you store it, or mould can grow on it.
- Store it loosely in a dry place, not packed tight in its bag.
- Clean it with cold water and a soft brush. Never use a washing machine.
- Keep the zips clean, and close them before you fold the tent.

### Sleeping bags

- Air your sleeping bag after every trip.
- Store it in the large cotton storage bag, not in the small stuff sack.
- Wash a down sleeping bag at most once a year, with a special down soap, at 30 degrees.
- Dry it in a tumble dryer on low heat with two or three clean tennis balls, so the down does not stick together.

### Waterproof jackets

Wash your waterproof jacket when it gets dirty, because dirt makes it less waterproof. Use a liquid soap, not powder, and no fabric softener. After washing, dry it in the tumble dryer on low heat; the heat helps the water-repellent finish work again. When water no longer forms drops on the fabric, use a spray-on proofing product.

## Your account and privacy

### Creating an account

With an account you can follow your orders, start returns, save addresses and see your warranty dates. To create one, click **Sign in** and then **Create account**. You only need an email address and a password.
