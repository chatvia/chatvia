1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
-------------------
ACME Corporation is a diversified industrial and consumer-products
company headquartered in Rotterdam, Netherlands. Founded in 1952, ACME
designs and manufactures industrial equipment, consumer gadgets, and
safety products sold to customers in more than 60 countries.
