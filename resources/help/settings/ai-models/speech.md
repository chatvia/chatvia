Speech to text turns voice input, and audio and video sources, into text.

- **Speech to text** — Scaleway AI or OpenAI; both use a Whisper model. "Automatic" uses Scaleway AI when it is connected, else OpenAI. "Off" turns these off and hides audio and video files from the sources.
- **Scaleway AI model** and **OpenAI model** (under **Advanced**) — The Whisper model each one uses. Leave them blank for the defaults.

**Tip:** Not sure? Keep "Automatic".
