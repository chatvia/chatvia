Spectra always keeps the counts, costs and times of each AI call. These switches decide what else it saves.

- **Prompts** — The messages sent to the AI. They include what visitors and chatters write.
- **System prompts** — The instructions of each agent, with the parts of your knowledge found for the question.
- **Replies** — What the AI answered.
- **Tools** — The tools the AI was offered for the call. They can be long.
- **Request data** — The whole request sent to the AI service. It holds the prompts again, plus settings such as the temperature.
- **Embeddings** — The numbers of each embedding. They are large and rarely useful, so they are off by default.

A change applies to the next AI call. Calls already in the log keep what was saved with them.

**Careful:** With prompts, replies or request data saved, the log holds personal details from conversations. Turn **Prompts**, **Replies** and **Request data** off when counts and costs are enough, and keep the number of days short.
