Pinewood Outdoor: terms of sale, Trail Club, rental and Second Trail

This document holds the rules that apply when you buy, rent or sell back gear at Pinewood Outdoor. It covers the Trail Club loyalty programme, our price promise, discounts, gift cards, business accounts, the rental terms, and Second Trail, our programme for used gear. The help centre answers the everyday questions; this text gives the full rules. Last updated: 1 March 2026.

1. Who we are

Pinewood Outdoor B.V. runs three shops and the online store at pinewood.example. Our office and warehouse are at 20 Mill Lane, in the industrial area north of the city. The warehouse is not a shop, and you cannot collect orders there; choose "Collect in a shop" instead.

2. Orders and contracts

A contract starts when we send the email that confirms your order, not when you click "Pay". If we cannot deliver an item, for example because the last one was damaged in the warehouse, we tell you within two working days and you do not pay for that item. You can order at most five of the same item in one order. For larger amounts, please ask our business team.

Prices on the website can change at any time, but the price in your confirmation email is the price you pay. If we make an obvious mistake in a price, such as a tent for 3.79 instead of 379, we may cancel that order line and refund what you paid.

3. Problems with a delivery

If a parcel arrives damaged, take a photo of the box before you open it, and tell us within 48 hours through the contact form. If a parcel has not arrived 10 working days after it left the warehouse, we treat it as lost and send the items again or refund you, as you prefer. You do not need to wait for the carrier's search.

4. Trail Club

Trail Club is our free loyalty programme. You join with your Pinewood account, in My account, or at the till in any shop. Members collect points and get extra services, depending on their level.

4.1 Points

You get 1 point for every euro you spend, in our shops and online. Rentals and repairs count too. Gift cards do not earn points when you buy them, only when you spend them. Every 500 points become a voucher of 10, which we put in your account automatically. Vouchers are valid for one year.

Points stay valid for two years after the purchase that earned them. When you return an item, we take the points for it back.

4.2 Levels

Your level depends on what you spent in the last twelve months.

Green: every member. Points, and an email on your birthday with a voucher of 5.

Silver: from 500 spent in twelve months. Everything in Green, plus free standard delivery on every order, without the minimum of 60, and early access to the summer sale.

Gold: from 1,500 spent in twelve months. Everything in Silver, plus free express delivery, 10% off all repairs in our workshop, and one free rental weekend per year for any item in the rental list.

We check your level on the first day of every month. If your spending drops below the level, you keep the level until the end of the next month.

5. Price promise

If you find the same product cheaper at another shop in the European Union within 14 days after you bought it from us, we pay you the difference. The product must be new, the same model and colour, and in stock at the other shop. Send us a link or a photo of the price through the contact form. The price promise does not apply to marketplaces where private people sell, to clearance or closing-down sales, or to prices that need a membership card at the other shop.

6. Discounts

Students get 10% off with a valid student card, in our shops only. Members of a hiking or scouting club that has a partner agreement with us get 15% off; ask your club for the code. Discounts cannot be combined with each other or with sale prices, and they do not apply to gift cards, rentals or gas cartridges.

Our summer sale starts on the first Monday of July and runs for four weeks. Our winter sale starts on 27 December.

7. Gift cards

Gift cards are valid for three years from the day you buy them. You can use a gift card in our shops and online, and you can pay with more than one gift card at once. Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash, and we cannot replace a lost gift card unless you bought it online with your account. You can check the balance on the back of the card with the link, or at any till.

8. Business accounts

Companies, schools and clubs can open a business account. With a business account you can pay by invoice; the invoice is due 30 days after delivery. From 2,000 spent in one order, business customers get 5% off. We can print a logo on backpacks and clothing from 20 pieces; printing takes about three weeks.

9. Rental terms

You can rent gear in the Lakeside and city centre shops. The prices are in the help centre. These rules apply to every rental.

9.1 Who can rent

You must be at least 18 years old and show an identity card or passport. Members of Trail Club can book online up to three months ahead; others can book up to one month ahead.

9.2 Deposit

We take a deposit when you pick the gear up: 50 for each tent and 30 for each other item. You get the deposit back when you return the gear in good condition. You can pay the deposit by card only, not in cash.

9.3 Pick-up and return

A weekend rental starts on Friday from 14:00 and ends on Monday before 12:00. A week rental runs for seven days from the day you pick the gear up. If you return the gear late, we charge 50% of the weekend price for every day late.

9.4 Cleaning and damage

Please return the gear dry and roughly clean. We clean every item ourselves, but if a tent comes back with mud inside or a sleeping bag smells of smoke, we charge a cleaning fee of 15. If something breaks while you use it normally, you pay nothing. If gear is damaged by misuse, such as burns from a stove inside the tent, we charge the repair price from our workshop list, up to the value of the deposit. Lost items are charged at the current shop price, minus 30%.

9.5 Rent first, buy later

If you buy the same product within 30 days after the rental, we take the rental price off the buying price. This also works for rentals paid with a Trail Club free weekend, but then there is nothing to take off.

10. Second Trail: selling your used gear

With Second Trail we buy back used Pinewood gear in good condition and sell it again at a lower price. This keeps gear in use for longer. We buy tents, sleeping bags, backpacks and jackets of our own brand that are at most six years old. We do not buy underwear, socks, base layers, sleeping mats or cooking gear.

10.1 Grades and what you get

The workshop checks every item and gives it a grade.

Grade A: like new, used only a few times, no repairs needed. You get 40% of the current shop price as store credit, or 30% in money.

Grade B: used, clean, with small signs of wear, works fully. You get 25% as store credit, or 18% in money.

Grade C: needs a small repair, such as a new zip or a patch. You get 10% as store credit. We do not pay money for grade C.

Store credit goes on a gift card or, for Trail Club members, into your account. Money is paid to your bank account within ten working days.

10.2 Where to bring it

You can bring Second Trail items to the Lakeside shop only, because the workshop is next door. Please wash sleeping bags and jackets first. If we cannot buy an item, you can take it back, or leave it with us and we give it to a charity that supports youth camps.

10.3 Buying second-hand gear

Second Trail gear is sold in the Second Trail corner of the Lakeside shop and in a separate section of the online store. It has a warranty of one year, not the normal three or five years. You can return it within 30 days, like any other item.

11. Your data

We keep your order history for as long as you have an account. If you do not log in or buy anything for three years, we send you an email, and if you do not answer within 30 days we delete the account. We keep invoices for seven years, because the law asks us to. Trail Club points expire when the account is deleted.

12. Complaints

If you are not happy with how we handled something, write to us through the contact form and choose "Complaint". A team lead answers within five working days. If we cannot solve it together, you can go to the European online dispute resolution platform.
