## Sleeping bags

Every sleeping bag has three temperatures, measured by an independent lab with the EN ISO 23537 test. The comfort temperature is the lowest temperature at which a person who sleeps cold still sleeps well. The limit temperature is the lowest at which a person who sleeps warm still sleeps well, curled up. Below the extreme temperature there is a risk to health. We advise you to choose by the comfort temperature.

### Synthetic or down?

Down is lighter and packs smaller for the same warmth, and it lasts longer when you care for it well. Synthetic fill keeps more of its warmth when it gets wet, dries faster, and costs less. For wet trips by boat or bike, or for a first sleeping bag, we advise synthetic. For long walks where every gram counts, we advise down.

All our down comes from farms that are checked against the Responsible Down Standard. No down is taken from live birds.

## Sleeping mats

A sleeping mat keeps the cold ground away from you, and it matters as much as the sleeping bag. The R-value tells you how well a mat holds warmth: the higher the number, the warmer the mat. For summer, an R-value of 2 is enough. For spring and autumn, choose at least 3. On snow, choose 5 or more.

| Model | Type | R-value | Thickness | Weight | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Foam Pad | Closed-cell foam | 2.0 | 1.5 cm | 0.4 kg | 25 |
| Air Lite | Air | 2.8 | 6 cm | 0.45 kg | 99 |
| Air Warm | Air with insulation | 4.6 | 7 cm | 0.6 kg | 159 |
| Comfort 7 | Self-inflating | 5.8 | 7.5 cm | 1.9 kg | 129 |

The air mats come with a pump sack, so you do not need to blow them up with your mouth. Breath brings moisture into the mat, and that can lead to mould inside. Every air mat comes with a small repair kit.

### Finding the right size

The back length is the distance from the bony point at the base of your neck to the top of your hip bones. Below 45 cm, take S/M; from 45 cm, take M/L. The Ridge 60 has a back system you can move up and down, so it fits back lengths from 40 to 55 cm. The Ridge 60 Women has a shorter range, a narrower shoulder harness and a hip belt shaped for women's hips.

Our shop staff can measure your back length and fill the backpack with sandbags, so you can feel the weight before you buy.
