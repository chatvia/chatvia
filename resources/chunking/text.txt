Pinewood Outdoor: returns, warranty and repairs

This text explains what you can do when a Pinewood product is not right for you, or when it breaks. It covers returns, refunds, the warranty and our repair workshop. Please read it before you contact our support team, because most answers are here.

Returns

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and clean, and it must be in its original packaging with all labels still on it. We cannot take back gift cards, underwear, socks or personalised items, because we cannot sell them again.

To start a return, sign in to your account and open My orders. Choose the order, click Return an item, and tell us which items you send back and why. Print the return label, stick it on the parcel, and drop the parcel at any post office or at one of our shops. You do not need to pay for the return inside the European Union. From other countries, the return costs 7.95, and we take this amount from your refund.

If you bought the item in one of our shops, you can also bring it back to any shop. Please bring the receipt or the order number. The shop staff check the item while you wait, and you get the refund at once.

Refunds

We check every return within three working days after it arrives at our warehouse. When the check is done, we send you an email and start the refund. The money goes back to the payment method you used for the order. A card refund takes five to ten working days to show on your statement, depending on your bank. A PayPal refund usually arrives within one day. A gift card payment goes back to the same gift card.

We refund the delivery costs only when the item was faulty or when we sent you the wrong item. In all other cases, the delivery costs stay with you.

Exchanges

We do not exchange items directly. To change a size or a colour, return the item and place a new order. This is the fastest way to get the item you want, because we do not have to wait for the return to arrive before we send the new one.

The warranty

Every Pinewood product has a warranty of three years against faults in the materials and in the making. Tents and backpacks have a warranty of five years, because we build them to last for many seasons. The warranty starts on the day of delivery, and you can see the end date of each product in your account.

The warranty does not cover normal wear, such as a faded colour or a worn sole. It also does not cover damage from accidents, or damage that comes from wrong use or wrong care. For example, a tent that was stored wet and got mould is not covered.

When something breaks, send us a photo of the problem and your order number through the contact form on our website. We usually answer within one working day. When the fault is covered, we repair the product for free. If a repair is not possible, we replace the product with the same one, or with a similar one if it is no longer sold.

Repairs after the warranty

Our repair workshop also repairs products after the warranty has ended, and it repairs products from other brands too. We can replace zips, close seams, change buckles, fix tent poles and patch holes in the fabric. Before we start, we send you a price, and we only start the work when you agree.

A new zip on a jacket costs 25, and a new zip on a tent door costs 35. A broken section of a tent pole costs 12. A small hole in the fabric costs from 10, depending on its size and the fabric. Most repairs take one to two weeks. In the busy summer months, a repair can take up to three weeks.

To send a product for repair, fill in the repair form in your account, pack the product in a box, and send it to our workshop with the form inside. You can also leave it at the Lakeside shop, which is next to the workshop.

Questions

If your question is not answered here, our support team is happy to help. You can reach us by chat on our website every day from 8:00 to 22:00, by email at any time, or by phone from Monday to Friday, from 9:00 to 17:00. Please have your order number ready. It starts with PW and has six numbers, for example PW-104233.
