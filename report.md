## 1. Executive Summary

Acme Inc. closed fiscal year 2025 with total revenue of **$1.482 billion**, a **17.4%** increase over the **$1.262 billion** reported in fiscal year 2024. Net income reached **$214.6 million**, up from **$168.3 million** the prior year, representing a net profit margin of **14.5%**.

The company added **312 new enterprise customers** during the year, bringing the total active customer base to **8,940 accounts** across 47 countries. Headcount grew from **6,180** employees at the start of the year to **7,420** employees by December 31, 2025.

Key highlights for 2025:

- Annual recurring revenue (ARR) crossed **$1.1 billion** for the first time, reaching **$1.13 billion** in December 2025.
- The flagship **AcmeCloud Platform** generated **$642 million** in revenue, accounting for **43.3%** of total company revenue.
- Gross margin improved to **68.2%**, up 210 basis points from **66.1%** in 2024.
- The company repurchased **$95 million** of its own stock and paid **$48 million** in dividends.
- Customer net revenue retention (NRR) held strong at **118%**.

## 10. Risk Factors & Challenges

Key risks identified for 2026 and beyond:

1. **Competitive pressure** — Increased competition in the cloud software market from larger incumbents.
2. **Supply chain dependency** — **38%** of hardware components sourced from a single region, creating concentration risk.
3. **Currency fluctuation** — **49.9%** of revenue generated outside North America, exposing the company to foreign exchange volatility.
4. **Talent retention** — High demand for AI engineering talent driving up compensation costs.
5. **Regulatory** — Evolving data privacy regulations across the 47 countries where Acme operates.
